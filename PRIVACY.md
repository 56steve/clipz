# Privacy Policy for Clipz

**Last updated: August 27, 2026**

Clipz ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for the Clipz desktop application.

## 1. Zero Data Collection & Transmission
Clipz is designed to run 100% locally on your personal device. 
- **No Remote Servers**: Clipz does not send, upload, sync, or transmit any user data, clipboard contents, IP addresses, or personal information to any external server, cloud service, or third party.
- **No Analytics or Telemetry**: Clipz contains no tracking, telemetry, or user behavior analytics software.

## 2. Local Data Storage
- All clipboard history items, timestamps, and application settings are stored locally on your machine in an SQLite database file (`%APPDATA%\clipz\clipz.db`).
- Sensitive items (such as password manager copies or credential patterns) are held strictly in volatile RAM with automatic 60-second cleanup (TTL) and are never saved to disk in plain text.

## 3. Data Control & Deletion
You retain complete control over your local data. You can clear your clipboard history at any time through the application settings or by deleting the local database file.
